# Review 2026-11-13: unrecorded priority issues

Source: a static review at `3afd663`. It found three issues. No build or GPU run supported
the findings.

| ID | Priority | Issue | Contract | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | P1 | Guarded dispatches in different encoders share the last bounds | PI15 Rev 4 | Fixed |
| R2 | P2 | Released handles keep their ownership-index entry until instance release | L11 Rev 1 | Fixed |
| R3 | P2 | The host sampler ignores descriptor address modes | CL5 Rev 1 | Fixed |

## R1

Confirmed by code reading: `writeGuard` in `lib/typegpu.ts` writes the shared guard buffers
through the queue, and the trap compares only the last encoder wrapper. The fix records a
staging copy inside the encoder (PI15 Rev 4). `ComputePipeline` cannot hold the device at pin
`b9bef6f`. The three rejected forms and their diagnostics are in language-request.md R41.

## R3

`Sampler` carries `addressModeU` and `addressModeV`. `Texture2d.sampleLevel` applies clamp,
repeat, or mirror-repeat per axis. A `compare` descriptor gives a sampler that traps with TX3.

Programs: `b27-sampler-address` (host samples for both axes and both modes, negative
coordinates, coordinates above one, defaults), `x26-live-sampler-address` (GPU against host).
Demonstrated red: against the previous runtime, `b27` printed `repeat(1.25,0.5)=0,0,1,1` and
`FAIL repeat`, and the sampler trap test failed with `did not trap` for the `compare` rows.

Evidence: `tools/gate.sh --require-backend` green, 431.58 s, 354 passed, 1 ignored (live).
`tools/live.sh`, every `x` program `PASS`: Metal (yawgpu) 186 s, Dawn 171 s.

## R2

Each index entry holds the instance and a reference count. Every generated child `*_release`
export subtracts one before the backend release. Synchronous owned getters
(`device_get_queue`, both `get_bind_group_layout` exports) now record an acquisition through the
generator's sync pattern. Surface handles and their views stay out of the index. They cannot
produce a device or a buffer, the two index readers.

Demonstrated red: the new facade backend test left 67 entries against a baseline of three after
one released batch. After the fix, four batches of 32 buffers and 32 command encoders restore
the baseline with the instance live.

Evidence: `tools/gate.sh --require-backend` green, 434.34 s, 359 passed, 1 ignored (live).
`tools/live.sh` on Metal (yawgpu): every `x` program `PASS`, 189 s.

## R1 fix

R41 landed as subscript §274, and the pin moved to `8638c8d`. `createComputePipeline` stores a
function that captures the device. Each guarded dispatch creates a 16-byte staging buffer,
writes the bounds to it through the queue, and records a copy into each guard buffer before
the pass. The same-encoder trap and its fixture `guarded-second-dispatch` are removed.

Programs: `x16-live-guarded` adds encoders A, B, A with counts 1, 3, 6 and one encoder with
counts 2 and 5, all before one submit, each checked against the host. `b15-guarded-dispatch`
adds the same shapes on Noop.
Demonstrated red: the new `x16` against the previous `lib/typegpu.ts` on Metal (yawgpu) printed
`FAIL batch=0 dispatch=0 slot=1 value=101 expected=999`.

Evidence: `tools/gate.sh --require-backend` green, 387.37 s, 358 passed, 1 ignored (live).
`tools/live.sh`, every `x` program `PASS`: Metal (yawgpu) 157 s, Dawn 155 s.
`tools/window.sh <example> --frames 30` on Metal: `boids` and `confetti` exit 0 with no `FAIL`.
